4. Why We Use Your Data

We use your data only for the following purposes:

  • To create and secure your account and verify you with an OTP
  • To store and show your homes, rooms and appliances, and keep them in sync across your devices
  • To calculate and display warranty status and send expiry alerts
  • To let household members you add see and manage the home
  • To show service providers near your pincode or location
  • To send your booking request to the provider you choose
  • To send notifications and service messages
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests and complaints
  • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse and security issues
  • To improve the App, fix bugs and develop new features
  • To comply with legal obligations

We will not use your data for a purpose that is materially different from these without asking for your consent again.

5. Your Consent

We process your personal data based on your consent, which you give by accepting this Policy and by allowing device permissions (camera, location, contacts, notifications). You can withdraw consent at any time by turning off permissions in your device settings or by deleting your account. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before it, and some features will stop working.

8. Service Provider Listings

The nearby service providers shown in the App (name, phone, address, rating) are drawn from publicly available and third-party sources and matched to your pincode or location. Your pincode or approximate coordinates are used on our servers to fetch these results. We do not pass your name or contact details to any provider unless you tap “Book”.

9. Data Stored on Your Device

To load quickly and work with a weak connection, the App saves a copy of your homes, rooms and appliance list on your device. Logging out clears this copy. If you uninstall the App, local data is removed from your device, but your account data on our servers remains until you delete your account.

If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.
