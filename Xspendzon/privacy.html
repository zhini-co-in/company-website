4. Why We Use Your Data

We use your data only for the following purposes:

  • To create and secure your account and verify you with an OTP
  • To store and show your expenses and keep them in sync across your devices
  • To read invoices with OCR, route expenses for approval and record approvals
  • To calculate and display totals, summaries and reports, including GST and budget reports for your organisation
  • To send notifications and service messages
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests and complaints
  • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse and security issues
  • To improve the App, fix bugs and develop new features
  • To comply with legal obligations

We will not use your data for a purpose that is materially different from these without asking for your consent again.

10. Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256), separate encryption for each organisation's data, token-based authentication, audit logs, and restricted access to our systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please protect your phone, keep your OTPs private, and log out on shared devices.

If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law, and we aim to do so within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.
